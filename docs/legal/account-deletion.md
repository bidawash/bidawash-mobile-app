# Delete your BidaWash account

You can permanently delete your BidaWash account and all associated
personal data at any time. This page explains both ways to do it.

## Option 1 — Delete from the app (fastest)

1. Open the BidaWash app and sign in.
2. Tap the **Account** tab (bottom right).
3. Scroll to the **Account** section.
4. Tap **Delete account**.
5. Confirm twice. You will be signed out immediately.

The deletion takes effect right away. There is nothing else you need
to do.

## Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email us at
[support@bidawash.com](mailto:support@bidawash.com) from the email
address associated with your account. Use the subject line
**"Delete my BidaWash account"**.

We will delete your account within 30 days and reply once it is
done.

## What gets deleted

- Your name, email, and phone number.
- Your account credentials.
- Any service or preference records tied to your account.

Encrypted backups roll off within 30 days.

## What may be kept

- Anonymized diagnostic data (crash and performance reports) that
  cannot be traced back to you.
- Financial or tax records we are legally required to keep (currently
  none — the app does not process payments).
- Records of prior support communication, retained separately for
  security and fraud-prevention purposes.

## Questions

Email support@bidawash.com and we will get back to you within one
business day.
